---
title: Miso Black Cod
image: /uploads/miso-black-cod.jpg
intro: "Black cod, when cooked right, has a very rich and sweet taste that pairs
  well with umami flavors. This miso marinated cod dish tastes best when
  marinated 24-48 hours but also tastes great the same day. I first introduced
  this dish back on Christmas of ‘24 as a part of a family tasting menu I did
  and it’s been a staple for any dinner parties/other tasting menus I have
  hosted since then. Pro tip: If you’re in a rush, just quickly broil and it’s
  ready to eat but if you have the time, I like to pat the miso off before
  broiling, and then once it’s ready, take it out and smear some sauce I put to
  the side and torch to give it a nice charred flavor."
media:
  - type: image
    src: /uploads/miso-black-cod.jpg
ingredients:
  - Black cod
  - Green onions
  - White miso
  - Sake (or mirin)
  - Maple syrup (or preferred sweetener)
  - Sesame oil
instructions:
  - Cut Cod into desired portions and put in a tray. (I buy whole filets from
    Costco which yields about 3-4 portions with some trimmings that I also
    marinate and eat as a taste test or snack while I cook other food haha)
  - In a pan, put a ratio of 3:1:1 (miso, sake, maple syrup) and bring to a
    simmer. Turn off heat and add a splash of sesame oil and let it cool. Take
    some of the sauce and save it for step 6.
  - Thinly slice green onion and soak in ice cold water. This will get rid of
    the spiciness and give a nice crisp texture which you can use as a garnish
    at the end.
  - Once the sauce is COMPLETELY cooled down, pour over cod and make sure each
    piece is coated. Lay a paper towel over the fish and put it in the fridge to
    marinate 24-48 hours.
  - When ready to cook, pat off miso and get the cod as dry as possible before
    putting it in the oven. If using a countertop oven, set temp to 350F and
    broil. If using an electric oven, set it to low broil. Time will vary, but
    you want it to reach about 130-135F. If you don’t have a way to check temp,
    I usually end up going for about 8-13 minutes.
  - Once ready, take it out and brush some sauce over the fish. If you have a
    torch, give it a quick flash to get some nice char marks. If not, you can
    put it back in the oven at the max temp or high setting and WATCH carefully
    until you get the desired char marks.
  - Plate and add green onion garnish and that’s it. Enjoy!!
