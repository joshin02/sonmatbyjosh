intro: Aglio means garlic in Italian and olio is oil. This dish may seem very
  bland at first sight and gives off greasy and unhealthy vibes but trust me, if
  done right, this might be one of the best pastas you’ll ever taste. Unlike
  traditional aglio olio, I prefer to emulsify the oil into a creamy sauce.
  Definitely a dish that will take some practice and even most restaurants I’ve
  ordered this from aren’t able to really do the dish justice. The good news
  (kinda), is that the ingredients used are super cheap so even if you mess up a
  few times, it won’t hurt your pockets LOL.

ingredients:
  - Olive oil
  - Crushed red peppers
  - Rosemary
  - Salt
  - Spaghetti
  - Whole peeled garlic cloves
  - "Pasta water "
  - Cherry tomatoes (optional)
instructions:
  - The first step is to boil your pasta. Thinner pasta is best but you can use
    whatever noodles you prefer. Also, don’t oversalt your pasta water for this
    recipe because it’ll be used to season the sauce later (aim for water that
    is slightly saltier than you would prefer in a soup)
  - As the noodles boil, we’ll flavor our oil. Pour olive oil into a pan (a
    little less than about the amount you would use to shallow fry) along with a
    stick of rosemary and 4-5 palm crushed whole garlic cloves. Put on low-med
    heat and cook until the garlic is cooked through. (Be careful not to burn
    the garlic as it will give your final sauce a bitter taste, go low in heat
    if in doubt) Turn off the heat and add crushed red peppers immediately while
    the oil is still hot and let it infuse while it cools.
  - Take a handful of cherry tomatoes and slice in half to also be added. This
    will cut through the oil sauce and give a nice sweet acidity to the dish.
  - Time to assemble the sauce! In a blender, put the oil with everything except
    the rosemary (you can pick off the rosemary leaves if you want). Put about a
    small ladle of the pasta water (the starch from it will thicken the sauce
    and the saltiness will season it). Start blending and you’ll see the oil and
    water/broth start to emulsify and become a super rich and creamy sauce. (the
    more pasta water you add, the more watery it’ll become so be careful with
    how much you’re adding)
  - To finish off the dish, add some noodles to a pan along with sauce and clams
    and quickly toss on high heat for 30 seconds to a minute. (Don’t
    overheat/cook in the pan or the sauce could end up breaking leading to a
    greasy pasta)
  - Plate and enjoy :))
